# ANIKA Initiative Website

> **Silence kills. Art airs.** Changing the world, Art at a time.

The public website for **ANIKA Initiative**, a Pan-African art-based initiative that uses artistic expression, dialogue and people-centred creative approaches to bring lived realities into the open. This is a Moringa School capstone project built for the organisation as a free digital platform.

---

## About the project

ANIKA Initiative began in 2015 in Nairobi as a gathering of seven poets and rappers and grew into an initiative working across five thematic areas: **Arts & Culture, Youth & Migration, Expressions, Gender Equality, and Governance**.

This repository holds the **public-facing website**, the place where the community can learn about ANIKA's work, browse and register for events, read impact stories, view the gallery, and give via M-Pesa.

**Pages**

- **Home**: hero, about, thematic areas, and calls to action
- **Programs**: the five thematic areas with methodologies
- **Events**: upcoming events with a category filter and registration
- **Impact**: verified proof points and case studies
- **Stories**: narratives from the field
- **Gallery**: photography from ANIKA's work
- **Get Involved**: attend, partner, or support
- **Donate**: one-off giving via M-Pesa
- **About**: brand story, vision & mission, values, and partners

## Brand system

The design follows ANIKA's brand guide.

**Colours**

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coral | `#EB4C47` | Primary accent, campaigns, emphasis |
| Green | `#389A51` | Community, growth, positive impact |
| Gold | `#E8A850` | Arts & culture, highlights |
| Blue | `#3A7599` | Dialogue, governance, institutional |
| Ink | `#1A1208` | Text, dark grounds |
| Cream | `#EDE8DF` | Backgrounds, negative space |

Working ratio: roughly **70% neutral, 20% primary accent, 10% secondary**.

**Typography**

- **Anton**: display and campaign headings ("Challenge")
- **Montserrat**: body, UI, captions ("Explain")
- **Cormorant Garamond**: quotes, editorial, poetry ("Feel")

**Voice:** Bold. Human. Expressive. Provocative. Hopeful.
**Ethical rule:** *Open, never expose.*
